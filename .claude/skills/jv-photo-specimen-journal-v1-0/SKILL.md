# 摄影标本日志 · Photo Specimen Journal v1.1

- keep the primary photographic fragment truthful to the source photo;
- anchor the main scene fragment using raw hand-torn fibrous paper edges with exposed cream paper fringe;
- replace abstract hand-drawn illustrations with clean, precise **archival field-journal annotations** (dotted trajectory lines with compass N/S/E/W labels, vertical survey measurement scale lines with tick marks, small yellow accent blocks ■, micro archival coordinates and serial numbers);
- include a small secondary photo disk cutout as an additional context specimen floating on the paper;
- format micro-text with **one concise poetic English title** + **one Chinese sub-caption with serial number**;
- output a single standalone poster image without top/bottom original photo split or diptych frame.

Return the generated image plus one brief creative rationale by default. Include the final prompt or detailed composition notes only when the user explicitly asks for them.

## Read the Photograph First

Build an internal **Scene Card**:

- **Core subject / Primary fragment:** the 1–2 key elements (person, landmark, vehicle, mountain peak, architectural eave) that anchor the photographic specimen.
- **Decomposition candidate:** select the primary fragment shape (horizontal strip, silhouette contour cutout, or organic landscape slice).
- **Secondary specimen disk:** identify one contextual detail from the scene (sky color, texture, secondary subject, or vegetation) to extract as a small circular photo disk cutout.
- **Spatial invariants:** key orientation, horizon, facing direction, or dominant gesture that must survive in the photographic fragment.
- **Trajectory path & compass orientation:** spatial direction (N, S, E, W) derived from the photo's movement, gaze, or path.
- **Survey scale placement:** vertical alignment axis alongside the photographic fragment suitable for tick marks and survey numbers.
- **Archival coordinates & serial:** field note metadata (LAT/LONG, timestamp/date, specimen serial e.g., `SPECIMEN NO. 01 / 04`).
- **Poetic English title & Chinese caption:** concise English name + Chinese reflective phrase.

Treat the main photo fragment as factual specimen evidence. Treat the paper annotations as an archival document system framing that evidence.

## Minimal Abstraction & Specimen Decomposition Engine

### Photographic Truth Retention

Unlike painterly or abstract illustration styles, the **Photo Specimen Journal** retains 100% photographic realism inside its photo fragments:

- Keep the main photo fragment un-stylized: true photographic lighting, grain, saturation, and natural color atmosphere.
- Never convert the main photo fragment into vector graphics, watercolor, halftone dots, or line drawings.
- The "abstraction" occurs exclusively through **physical paper decomposition**: tearing, cutting, isolating, and arranging photographic fragments on the paper field.

### Four Specimen Decomposition Modes

Choose one decomposition mode based on the source photo geometry:

1. **Mode A: Horizontal Strip Panorama (全景横向撕条)**
   - *Best for:* Expansive landscapes, mountain ranges, horizons, coastal lines.
   - *Treatment:* The scene is torn into 1–2 wide horizontal photographic strips with ragged top and bottom hand-torn fibrous paper edges. The core subject (peak, boat, horizon) is centered or slightly offset within the strip.

2. **Mode B: Silhouette Cutout + Background Strip (主体剪影 + 背景撕条)**
   - *Best for:* People cycling, running, walking, or standing against open fields/sky.
   - *Treatment:* The upper subject contour is cleanly cut out along body/vehicle lines, while the lower base sits inside a hand-torn landscape strip. The subject physically breaks out of the torn strip boundary onto the warm ivory paper.

3. **Mode C: Subject Cutout + Secondary Context Disk (主体剪切 + 细节圆片)**
   - *Best for:* Solitary figures, close-up subjects, camping setups, architectural details.
   - *Treatment:* The primary subject forms an irregular torn photographic island in the center, while a small secondary circular photo disk cutout floats to the upper-right or side, highlighting a macro detail (e.g., foliage, stone, sky gradient).

4. **Mode D: Organic Landscape Contour Slice (有机地貌轮廓切片)**
   - *Best for:* Winding rivers, coastline curves, overlapping mountain ridges, trail paths.
   - *Treatment:* The photograph is sliced following natural contours (river curve, hill ridge), creating 2 overlapping or separated organic torn photographic fragments.

## Archival Field-Journal Annotation System

Replacing the abstract hand-drawn illustration field of standard zines, the paper canvas around the photo fragments is populated with a delicate, precise **Archival Field-Journal Annotation System**:

1. **Dotted Trajectory Lines (虚线方位轨迹)**
   - Fine, thin dotted lines (`········`) extending horizontally or diagonally across the paper field.
   - Interspersed with compass directions and key markers: `N ········ S`, `W ········ K ········ E`, or `TRAJECTORY 01 → 02`.
   - Drawn in faint charcoal gray or muted graphite ink.

2. **Vertical Survey Measurement Scale Lines (垂直测量刻度尺)**
   - A crisp, delicate vertical axis line alongside the main photo fragment.
   - Marked with fine perpendicular tick marks and small mechanical numbers (`00`, `05`, `10`, `15`, `20`, `mm` or `cm`).
   - Evokes scientific field survey sheets, botanical specimen logs, or cartographic tools.

3. **Small Yellow Accent Blocks (■ 微型黄色色块标贴)**
   - 1–2 tiny, high-chroma solid yellow square accent blocks (`■`, approx. 3x3mm scale).
   - Placed near serial numbers or survey line endpoints (`■ SPECIMEN 01`).
   - Acts as a bright, quiet color anchor on the warm ivory paper without overwhelming the photographic truth.

4. **Micro Archival Coordinates & Metadata (微型地理坐标与档案编码)**
   - Small typewriter or monospace micro-text positioned near the paper margin or fragment corners.
   - Includes geographic coordinates: `LAT 34°12'15"N / LONG 108°55'40"E`.
   - Includes timestamp/serial: `FIELD NOTE NO. 2026.08 — SPECIMEN 01/04`.

5. **Secondary Photo Specimen Disk (圆形照片切片标本)**
   - A small circular photo cutout (disk shape) extracted directly from the source image.
   - Displayed as a secondary floating specimen on the ivory paper, framed with a fine line or slight paper edge depth.

## Photo–Paper Edge Handoff & Torn Edge Treatment

Treat the boundary of the main photo fragment as a physical hand-torn paper edge:

- **Hand-Torn Fibrous Fringe:** Irregular ripped edge with soft white paper fibers pulling away from the photograph, exposing the warm cream core paper stock.
- **Asymmetric Edge Contour:** Soft scallops, shallow tear notches, uneven rises, and occasional longer fiber strands along 40–80% of the photo perimeter.
- **Paper Layer Shadow:** A very shallow, diffuse paper depth shadow beneath the fragment, confirming its status as a physical paper cutout resting on the archival sheet.
- **Strict Avoids:** No clean vector clipping paths, no perfectly sharp digital square boxes, no heavy drop shadows, no curled paper corners, and no decorative sticker borders.

## Composition System & Layout Ranges

- **Canvas Format:** Vertical 3:5 ratio, single standalone poster image.
- **Photo Specimen Area:** Occupies 25–45% of the total canvas area.
- **Active Paper Field:** Occupies 55–75% of the total canvas area, providing generous breathing room for annotations and quiet paper negative space.
- **Focal Alignment:** Align the main photo fragment along the vertical lower-middle section or lower-third, letting trajectory lines and typography balance the upper negative space.

## Micro-Text & Typography System

Text in the Photo Specimen Journal functions as formal archival record-keeping combined with poetic field observation.

### 1. Primary English Title
- **Form:** Concise poetic title, short observed phrase, or keyword sequence.
- **Length:** 1 to 5 words maximum.
- **Font & Style:** Vintage typewriter monospace serif or spaced clean serif, all-caps or quiet title case.
- **Examples:** `THE ROAD CONTINUES`, `SILENCE OF THE RIDGE`, `WHEN THE LIGHT FADES`, `HORIZON / DRIFT / RAIN`, `THE LAKE DREAMS IN BLUE`.

### 2. Secondary Chinese Sub-Caption & Serial Number
- **Form:** One short evocative Chinese line paired with serial numbering.
- **Length:** 8 Chinese characters or fewer + serial code.
- **Font & Style:** Faint fine Songti (宋体), Heiti (黑体), or pencil handwriting.
- **Examples:** `阳光不漫长 — 01 / 04`, `白云沉，慢一点 — 03 / 04`, `山风抚溪流 — SPECIMEN 02`.

### Placement & Ink
- Positioned in quiet paper areas below or alongside the main photographic specimen.
- Ink color: Warm charcoal gray, graphite, or faded brown-black, fully integrated into the paper texture.
