# 摄影极简精粹 · Scene Essence Minimal v1.1

Turn a user-supplied photo into a single standalone poetic paper-poster artwork using the **gc-minimal-zine-poster-v0-1** prompt compiler architecture and **scene-distillation-zine-v1-3** source distillation methodology.

Core Philosophy: **Extract 1–3 core geometric shapes and spatial structures from complex reality, reduce them to minimal flat graphic forms or fine structural lines, float them on warm paper negative space with a single vivid color anchor, and present as a single standalone artwork.**

## Mode Policy

Use **Standard Mode** for all generation. Use the Standard Mode Prompt Compiler to convert the distilled visual elements from the photo into a compact, 4-paragraph, high-fidelity prompt. Single image output only (never a diptych, side-by-side, or combined with the original photo).

---

## Step 1: Scene Distillation Card (Source Analysis)

Before selecting variation recipes or compiling prompts, inspect the source photo to extract its essence:

- **Semantic Nucleus**: The central subject or core theme of the photograph.
- **Core Subjects (1–3 shapes)**: Identify 1 to 3 primary subjects. Extract their simplified geometric contours, silhouettes, or structural lines. Do NOT invent unrelated objects; do NOT keep photographic pixels.
- **Dominant Gesture & Spatial Structure**: Radial convergence (courtyard looking up), perspective axial slant (street view), vertical layering (tower/building), or horizontal skyline. Preserve this underlying structural skeleton.
- **Single High-Chroma Color Anchor**: Identify 1 dominant vivid accent hue from the source photo (e.g. vermilion red, cobalt blue, warm gold) to serve as the visual tension anchor.
- **Material Character & Rendering Style**: Hard/architectural (geometric blocks), structural/linear (fine ink lines), organic/fluid (soft watercolor wash), or radial geometry.
- **Poetic Title & Subtitle**: Extract a 2–5 word English title and an italic subtitle reflecting the scene's emotional resonance.
- **Discard List**: Delete realistic backgrounds, sky textures, crowds, clutter, photorealistic shading, and secondary details.

---

## Standard Mode Prompt Compiler

The prompt compiler translates the Scene Distillation Card into pure renderable image generation instructions.

### Visual Rules Used by the Prompt Compiler

- **Visual Identity**: Poetic minimal paper poster, 70%–90% negative space, aged warm ivory paper texture, small geometric shape cluster (8%–25%), sparse elegant serif typography, one high-chroma color anchor.
- **Core Visual Constraints**: Vertical 3:5 paper canvas, orthographic flat scanned appearance, non-photographic graphic simplification (flat shapes, fine ink lines, flat silhouettes).
- **Hard Exclusions**: No photographic pixels, no photo cutouts/collages, no diptych/split-screen layout, no commercial ad hierarchy, no 3D rendering.

### First-Principles Prompt Fields

Every prompt must answer these rendering questions in order:

1. **Canvas**: Tall vertical 3:5 phone-poster; full-frame aged warm ivory/beige paper; no outer wall border, no device mockup. Single poster canvas only.
2. **Attention Geometry**: 70%–90% empty paper; 1 visual subject cluster occupying 8%–25% of canvas; positioned center, lower-middle, upper-middle, or slightly off-center.
3. **Image Anchor**: Derived strictly from the source photo's 1–3 core geometric shapes, fine structural lines, or flat silhouettes. No photorealistic photo fragments; no arbitrary invented objects.
4. **Anchor Treatment**: Flat geometric color blocks, fine structural line drawing, organic brush stroke, flat vector-like silhouette, or radial perspective grid on warm absorbent paper. Softened printed edges or light risograph grain; NO photographic pixels or camera textures.
5. **Typography System**: Poetic English serif title (e.g., "Sky Held by Walls") + optional italic subtitle at lower-left or beneath the visual cluster; small monospaced/typewriter caption with archival date or coordinates.
6. **Color Logic**: Warm ivory/beige paper background + neutral grays/blacks for structural lines/secondary shapes + **ONE unmistakably high-chroma color anchor** (occupying 0.8%–2.5% of total canvas or 15%–35% of the visual cluster). Prefer cobalt blue, ultramarine, vermilion red, lemon yellow, pear green, magenta-pink, or bright orange.
7. **Reproduction Texture**: Flat orthographic scan of matte absorbent paper, faint risograph grain, slight letterpress ink bite, soft xerox degradation, no 3D depth, diffuse ambient light.
8. **Emotional Temperature**: Quiet, poetic, archival, meditative, Japanese/Korean minimal zine aesthetic.
9. **Hard Avoids**: Photo embedding, raw photo clippings, diptych, split canvas, commercial ads, bold logos, 3D graphics, cute cartoons, dense scrapbooking, neon lighting.

---

## Standard Color Engine

- Default to **one visibly saturated, opaque chromatic color anchor** derived from or complementing the source photo.
- Use explicit color wording: `fully saturated cobalt-blue risograph ink`, `opaque ultramarine geometric cutout`, `vivid vermilion red accent block`, `clean tomato-red printed silhouette`.
- Keep paper background, secondary shapes, lines, and text subdued in neutral tones (warm gray, charcoal, pale beige).
- The high-chroma anchor must occupy **0.8%–2.5% of the total canvas** (or **15%–35% of the core cluster**) and remain striking even at thumbnail scale.
- Never use weakening descriptors like `pale`, `muted`, `faded`, `pastel`, or `near-monochrome` for the color anchor.

---

## Standard Prompt Shape

Write the final prompt as **four compact paragraphs**:

1. **Canvas & Layout**: tall vertical 3:5 single paper poster, aged warm ivory paper texture, 70%–90% empty space, visual cluster size (8%–25%) and placement.
2. **Distilled Subject & Anchor Treatment**: simplified 1–3 geometric shapes / structural lines / flat silhouettes extracted from photo, specific rendering technique (geometric blocks, fine ink lines, radial perspective geometry), no photographic pixels.
3. **Typography & Color Anchor**: English serif title + italic subtitle, explicit high-chroma hue and material form (0.8%–2.5% canvas share), neutral ink secondary shapes, risograph/letterpress defects.
4. **Scan Texture & Hard Avoids**: flat orthographic scan, matte absorbent paper, quiet meditative mood, hard avoid-list (no photo fragments, no diptych, no 3D, no commercial clutter).

---

## Variation Engine

Select one option from each axis based on the source photo's characteristics:

### Layout Family (Single Image Output Only)
- **center-fragment**: small simplified shape cluster in the center with surrounding air
- **lower-left-float**: small geometric anchor floating in the lower-left quadrant
- **upper-right-block**: geometric color block in upper-right with loose text drift
- **irregular-cutout**: clean graphic paper cutout shape floating on ivory field
- **radial-perspective**: concentric or radial geometric arrangement (e.g. courtyard sky view)
- **single-specimen**: one isolated structural form with poetic title below

### Subject Simplification Technique (from jv v1.0)
- **Geometric Blocks**: flat geometric shapes filling soft muted tones (architecture, towers)
- **Structural Lines**: ultra-fine gray/charcoal structural lines (scaffolding, bridges, branches, rails)
- **Watercolor Wash**: soft diffuse color wash for atmospheric grounds or water
- **Organic Clusters**: loose ink brush dots/clusters for foliage or crowds
- **Radial / Perspective Geometry**: shapes aligned to perspective vanishing points or radial loops

### Typography Mode
- **serif-title-subtitle**: elegant serif English title with italic sub-caption at bottom left
- **archival-microtext**: poetic title with date/coordinates in typewriter font
- **drifting-phrase**: short poetic phrase pressing gently against the geometric anchor edge

### Texture Mode
- **risograph-grain**: subtle riso ink texture on absorbent paper
- **xerox-softness**: soft vintage photocopy texture
- **letterpress-bleed**: tactile ink bleed along graphic edges
- **flat-paper-scan**: clean matte paper fibers under diffuse scanner light

### Mood Mode
- **quiet-solitude**: calm, reflective, distant
- **architectural-clarity**: precise, structural, quiet
- **summer-afternoon**: warm, hazy, nostalgic
- **archival-memory**: subtle, poetic, time-worn

---

## Workflow

1. **Build Scene Distillation Card**: Parse the source photo to identify 1–3 core geometric shapes, spatial structure, and primary color accent. Create the discard list.
2. **Select Variation Recipe**: Pick Layout Family, Simplification Technique, Typography Mode, Texture Mode, and Mood Mode.
3. **Apply Standard Color Engine**: Select one high-chroma hue for the primary anchor; keep all other elements neutral.
4. **Compile 4-Paragraph Prompt**: Follow the Standard Prompt Shape precisely. Ensure clear prohibition of photographic pixels and diptychs.
5. **Generate Image**: Run image generation to produce a **single standalone artwork**.
6. **Return Output**: Present the generated image, final prompt, and concise Chinese creative rationale.

---

## Negative Constraints

Always avoid:
- Photographic pixels, photorealistic rendering, raw photo crops, or photo collages.
- Diptych, split-screen, side-by-side comparison, or displaying original photo alongside poster.
- Commercial headline hierarchy, ad layouts, brand logos, CTAs.
- Clean digital UI, bright pure white background, glossy paper mockups, heavy drop shadows.
- 3D graphics, cinematic lighting, neon colors, cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Cute cartoons, anime styling, kawaii elements, dense scrapbooking.
- Inventing subjects unrelated to the source photo.
